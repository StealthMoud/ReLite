## SoC / platform notes

UNISOC (formerly Spreadtrum) T7250 is part of the UMS9230 platform family.
Devices on this platform are generally:

- ARM64 (`arm64-v8a`), confirm via `ro.product.cpu.abilist`.
- Built against a UNISOC-provided BSP/vendor layer, distinct from
  Qualcomm/MediaTek vendor stacks — this affects which kernel source tree
  and which proprietary vendor blobs are relevant (see `research/kernel.md`).

## RAM Expansion

"RAM Expansion" (also seen as "Extended RAM" / "Virtual RAM" across OEM
skins) is a storage-backed swap-like extension, not physical RAM and not
Android's built-in kernel zRAM. ReLite treats these as three distinct
mechanisms and never conflates them (master plan section 14):

1. **Physical RAM** — 6 GB, fixed in hardware.
2. **Kernel zRAM** — Android's standard compressed-RAM swap, managed by
   the kernel; ReLite does not touch this (`docs/safety.md`).
3. **OEM RAM Expansion** — a UI-toggleable feature that reserves storage
   as page-out space. Slower than physical RAM, and on eMMC/lower-tier
   UFS storage may increase wear and introduce latency spikes under
   memory pressure.

`relite tune ram-expansion probe` searches `settings global/secure/system`
for a confirmed key rather than guessing one — see
`relite/tuning.py::RAM_EXPANSION_CANDIDATE_KEYS` and
`devices/realme/RMX5303/findings.md` for what has actually been observed.
